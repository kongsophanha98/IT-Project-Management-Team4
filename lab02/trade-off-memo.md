# Trade-off memo · CR-03 · Pilot moves to week 12

To: Head of IT (sponsor) · From: PM (supervising lecturer) · Date: 2026-10-13 · Decision by: 2026-10-30 (end of W5)

## Fixed facts

- C-01 Pilot with 50 students; charter date is week 14, the dean's notice of 2026-10-10 asks for week 12.
- C-02 Team 4 x 10 h/week: capacity 480 h for 12 weeks (560 h for 14).
- C-03 Cash budget 200 USD, of which 85 USD is reserve.

## Request

The dean asks to shorten the semester by two weeks. Planned work is 504 h; capacity falls by 80 h to 480 h. So 24 h must leave the scope before any risk occurs, and contingency shrinks from 56 h to 48 h. Current classes: Must 300 h, Should 86 h, Could 106 h, Won't 12 h.

## Options

| Option                                   | Time     | Cost    | Quality at the pilot                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A Defer Could and Won't (F13-F17, 118 h) | 0 weeks  | 0 USD   | Must and Should intact (386 h); no media, chat, question bank; buffer 94 h |
| B Keep all scope, pay a TA for 24 h      | 0 weeks  | 288 USD | All 17 features; buffer 0 h; breaks C-03                                   |
| C Keep the pilot in week 14              | +2 weeks | 0 USD   | Scope intact; contradicts the dean's date, breaks C-01                     |

## Recommendation

A. It leaves a 94 h buffer, larger than the original 56 h, and touches no Must or Should feature. B costs 288 USD, 144 % of the whole cash budget. C is the dean's decision, not ours, and costs two weeks the semester no longer has.

## If no decision by 2026-10-30

The team continues on Must features only (300 h) and starts Should work in W6. The 24 h excess stays open and the 94 h buffer is not secured.
