# Lab-03: life cycle and methodology · Realtime Quiz

The project uses a hybrid life cycle: a predictive frame of initiation and planning (weeks 1-3), four two-week Scrum sprints (weeks 4-11), a hardening phase (weeks 12-13) and a pilot with 50 students (week 14). The profile score of the project falls in the hybrid band and the weighted comparison ranks the hybrid first, because the sponsor needs an approved plan by week 3 and a fixed pilot date while a first-time Scrum team with uncertain real-time technology needs short feedback loops. The reasoning, the consequences with mitigations and the tailoring are in [tailoring-decision-record.md](tailoring-decision-record.md).

The sponsor controls the project through four phase gates (G1 2026-10-19, G2 2026-11-16, G3 2026-12-14, G4 2027-01-04) with measurable checklists, stage tolerances for time, hours, scope, quality and risk, and management by exception: the PM escalates with a three-line exception report only when a tolerance is forecast to be exceeded. A weekly one-page highlight report and the sprint reviews give the sponsor and PM continuous visibility; closure is defined by acceptance, hand-over, lessons learned and administrative closure criteria in [governance.md](governance.md).
